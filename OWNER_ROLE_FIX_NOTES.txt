OWNER ROLE FIX — 2026-10-14

Verified production issue:
- /api/owner-bootstrap was being invoked successfully after the route repair.
- The admin update used camelCase appMetadata, but @netlify/identity admin.updateUser() accepts app_metadata for persisted role metadata.
- refreshSession() does not force rotation of a freshly issued JWT, so a newly persisted role can remain invisible in the current session.

Repair:
- Persist roles with app_metadata.roles.
- Assign member + staff + owner only to emails in RAMIRO_STAFF_EMAILS.
- Read the user back with admin.getUser() and fail if owner/staff did not persist.
- After successful first-time promotion, end the stale member session once and send the user back to /account/ for one clean sign-in, which issues a new JWT carrying the owner role.
- Regular member accounts are unaffected.
- No public homepage, menu, visual system, database schema, SEO, billing, contracts, finance, quotes, or customer data changes.
